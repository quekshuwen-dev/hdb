For flat owners

Selling your flat?

Understand the steps involved in selling your HDB flat before starting the process. A well-prepared seller has a smoother experience.

Start Intent to Sell View Seller Guide
1

Check your eligibility to sell

Confirm you have met the Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) and are eligible to list your flat for sale.

2

Register your Intent to Sell

Register your intention to sell your flat on the Flat Portal. This is required before you can proceed with the sale.

3

Prepare your flat for sale

Set a realistic asking price, prepare for viewings and understand the resale transaction process.

4

Grant Option to Purchase

Once you agree on a price with your buyer, grant the Option to Purchase (OTP) and complete the transaction.

5

Complete the resale transaction

Submit the resale application together with your buyer and proceed to flat handover.

Before you list — check these

Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) You must have lived in the flat for at least 5 years (MOP) before selling.
Outstanding CPF housing grant Check if you need to return any CPF housing grant to the CPF account upon sale.
Outstanding HDB loan Understand the remaining loan amount and how it will be settled upon completion of the sale.
Resale flat transactions Check recent transacted prices for similar flats in your area before setting your asking price.
Salesperson engagement Decide if you want to engage a CEA-registered salesperson to assist with the sale.
